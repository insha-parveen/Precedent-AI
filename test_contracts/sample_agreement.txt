MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2024 ("Effective Date") by and between Acme Corporation ("Client") and Beta Services LLC ("Provider").

Section 1. Services
1.1 Provider shall perform the services described in each Statement of Work ("SOW") executed by the parties.
1.2 Provider shall perform the services in a professional and workmanlike manner.

Section 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 Client shall pay Provider the fees set forth in each SOW.
2.2 Payment terms are Net 30 days from invoice date.
2.3 Late payments shall bear interest at 1.5% per month.

Section 3. Confidentiality
3.1 Each party agrees to hold the other's confidential information in strict confidence.
3.2 Confidential information includes all non-public business information.

Section 4. Term and Termination
4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for two (2) years.
4.2 Either party may terminate for convenience upon sixty (60) days written notice.
4.3 Either party may terminate for cause upon thirty (30) days written notice of material breach.

Section 5. Governing Law
5.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

Section 6. Limitation of Liability
6.1 Neither party's total liability shall exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
6.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

Section 7. Intellectual Property
7.1 All work product created by Provider shall be owned by Client.
7.2 Provider grants Client a perpetual, irrevocable license to use any pre-existing IP.

Section 8. Non-Solicitation
8.1 During the term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall solicit the other's employees.

Section 9. Force Majeure
9.1 Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond their reasonable control.

Section 10. General
10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
10.2 Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
